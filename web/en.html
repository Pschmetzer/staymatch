For Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on a computer · no account · not yet in the Chrome Web Store

This browser can't run the extension. You can still use the neighborhood finder here.

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it. On a Mac, a double-click is enough.
  2. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions into the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Open an Airbnb search and set your budget and must-haves in the panel. Done.
Features

Everything you need to find the right place faster.

For city breaks, workations, weekends away and any trip where you don't want to spend hours searching.

Your must-haves

Entire place, no professional host, Superhost or Guest favorite, enough reviews. Anything that doesn't match shows up as a warning.

Reviews, read for you

On the listing page, StayMatch pulls keywords from the reviews: clean, location, noisy, Wi-Fi. If you want "quiet" and other guests wrote "noisy", it warns you.

Reasons, not a black box

Every score comes with reasons: ✓ met, ⚠ doesn't fit. So you know why a listing is at the top.

Budget in view

Set a nightly budget right in the panel, or a total budget in the neighborhood finder. Listings that cost too much move down.

The right neighborhood (optional)

The neighborhood finder rates every area by your interests (nightlife, chilling, sightseeing, food, family, nature) using real places from OpenStreetMap.

Remembers what you've seen

History, 👍 and 👎, notes and the price change since your first visit. Anything you reject stays out of your top 3.

Why not just sort?

Airbnb sorts by price or stars. StayMatch sorts by you.

Airbnb search alone

  • You open dozens of listings to compare reviews, type and price.
  • A 5.0 from three reviews sits next to a 4.9 from 300.
  • Whether the area suits you is up to your own research.

With StayMatch

  • The three best matches are at the top, with reasons and warnings.
  • Stars are weighted by the number of reviews.
  • If you like, the neighborhood is already chosen by your interests.
How we score

Honest points instead of blind stars.

  • Stars weighted by count: 5.0 from three reviews counts for less than 4.9 from 300.
  • Price against your budget: listings that cost too much move down.
  • Your must-haves: met earns points, otherwise you get a warning.
  • Neighborhood (optional): the score from the neighborhood finder.
  • If a detail is missing, it's left out rather than counted as 0.

Great for groups too

Traveling with friends? Then fair for everyone.

Before you book

Checklist: 10 questions before you hit “Reserve”.

  • Checked the total price, with cleaning fee, service fee and taxes?
  • Enough real beds for everyone, sofa bed counted honestly?
  • Read the cancellation policy: free until when?
  • House rules fit: quiet hours, visitors, pets, smoking?
  • Check-in time and key handover fit your arrival?
  • Read the latest reviews, not just the average?
  • Checked the location on the map: way to transit, center or beach?
  • Amenities there: Wi-Fi, kitchen, washer, heating or AC?
  • Thought of costs on site: tourist tax, deposit, electricity?
  • Payment and messages only through Airbnb?

Suspect a scam? Here's what to do.
  1. Never pay or keep chatting outside Airbnb, even if someone asks you to.
  2. Check links: real pages are on airbnb.com, airbnb.de and so on.
  3. Report the listing or message to Airbnb.
  4. Already paid? Call your bank right away and report it to the police.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does StayMatch cost anything?

No. The extension and the neighborhood finder are free and open source under the MIT license.

Is StayMatch available in English?

This page is. The extension panel and the neighborhood finder are currently in German only. English versions are planned, but there's no date yet.

Is StayMatch made by Airbnb?

No. StayMatch is an independent project and not affiliated with Airbnb. The links lead to the regular search, and you book there as usual.

Does the extension read Airbnb in the background?

No. It only reads the page you opened yourself and makes no requests of its own. When you go to the next page, it scores the listings there. Airbnb may still view extensions reading its pages differently; the terms of service are theirs. If that bothers you, just use the neighborhood finder.

Why do I need to turn on Developer mode?

The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until then, you load it as an unpacked extension. You can review the code on GitHub at any time.

The extension doesn't show up. What now?

Check on the extensions page (see step 2) that StayMatch is switched on, then reload the Airbnb page. After an update, remove the old version and load the new folder. If that doesn't help, open an issue on GitHub.

What happens to my data?

Must-haves, likes, notes and history stay locally in your browser or the extension's storage. There's no account and no StayMatch server. In the neighborhood finder, the place name and your IP address go to OpenStreetMap's map services (place search, points of interest), and the map tiles come from there too. The font and map library are served from our own site, not from Google. The website sets no cookies and has no tracking. The quick search and the links on the city pages open Airbnb with your city, dates and guests in the address, and Airbnb’s own privacy rules apply there. Bug reports and issues go to GitHub (USA). Details in German: neighborhood finder, Airbnb links, GitHub.

How does StayMatch know what suits me?

From what you tell it: budget, must-haves for the place and, if you use the neighborhood finder, your interests for the area. On top of that come the stars weighted by number of reviews, and your 👍 and 👎. How that becomes a score is explained under How we score.

Can I search with friends?

Yes. Add your travel companions in the neighborhood finder, and StayMatch finds the area where everyone is happy and checks there are enough beds. Then share the link in your group chat.

Does StayMatch work on my phone?

The extension doesn't: browser extensions only run in Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer, not in the Airbnb app. You can use the neighborhood finder on your phone and send yourself the link for your laptop.

Which cities work?

Any city where OpenStreetMap knows the neighborhoods, so almost every larger city worldwide. The first search for a city can take up to a minute. After that, your browser keeps the data for a day.

Why only Airbnb?

Because it has lots of holiday rentals and its search can be opened via a link with dates and guests. Other sites like Booking.com are an idea for later, not a promise.

How accurate is the recommendation?

It's a good starting point, not a verdict. StayMatch always shows why a listing is at the top and warns you about things like too few beds or a price over budget.

Limits

What StayMatch can't do.

No Airbnb API, no booking

Airbnb has no public API. The extension only scores what's on the page you have open, and the neighborhood finder only uses map data. You book on Airbnb.

Search links may change

The parameters for dates, guests and price limit aren't officially documented. Whether the price limit is per night or total isn't confirmed. Check the filters on Airbnb.

Airbnb's page structure changes

The extension relies on the URL and structured data first and leaves out missing fields. So a card may sometimes end up without a score.

The map servers are sometimes slow

The public OpenStreetMap servers can take up to about a minute for a new city. Results stay in your browser for a day.

Something not working? Report a bug on GitHub or try the extension anyway.
