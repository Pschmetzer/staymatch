How this was calculated

  1. Data: OpenStreetMap, retrieved on 2026-11-14 via the Overpass API (overpass.private.coffee); 2,894 places after removing duplicates.
  2. Neighborhoods: entries that OpenStreetMap lists as a suburb, quarter or neighbourhood (65 in this city).
  3. Counting: for each neighborhood we count the places per category within 800 m. Fast food counts one third as much as a restaurant.
  4. Weighting: each trip type weighs the categories differently (table). The result is scaled to 0 to 100, relative to the densest choice in the city.
  5. Filtering: neighborhoods with too few places and those further than 6 km from the center drop out (60 remain). Two recommendations are at least 1.2 km apart.
