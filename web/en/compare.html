Which city fits us?

Choose who is travelling and what matters. StayMatch scores every pre-loaded city and shows which fits best, with the best neighbourhood there. The calculation runs only in your browser.

Who is travelling?

The first comparison downloads about 2.5 MB of compressed pre-computed city data (OpenStreetMap) once. After that every change applies instantly. Your input never leaves the browser.

    How we calculate

    • Each city is scored like in the neighbourhood finder: bars, parks, sights, restaurants, stations and more within 800 m of each neighbourhood.
    • For the city comparison one common yardstick applies to all cities. Cities with many mapped places look denser, and OpenStreetMap is sometimes thinner in small cities.
    • The city score (common yardstick) is 70% the best neighbourhood and 30% the average of the three best.
    • The price range is an estimate from location and sights, not real prices.
    • Neighbourhood points are relative to their own city, as in the app. “See neighbourhoods” opens the app (in German) with the city and type. Further people travel as a wish link in the fragment (after the #), not in the address.
